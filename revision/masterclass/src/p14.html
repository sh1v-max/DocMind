14.1Architecture

What problem does this application solve?Creating a structured, reviewed course (modules, lessons, quizzes) from a topic in minutes instead of days, with the human staying in control of the outline. Writing a structured course by hand takes days of outlining, drafting lessons and quizzes. The system turns a topic and a few parameters into a reviewed, structured course: the human controls the plan (outline approval), the machine does the bulk drafting, and quality checks catch obvious problems before publishing. The engineering problem is making a long, multi-step, failure-prone LLM process reliable and affordable. A trainer enters “Intro to RAG, beginner, 4 modules”; 3 minutes later approves an outline; 6 minutes later has 16 lessons and 4 quizzes in draft. Educators or teams creating training content; they need control (approval/editing) and trustworthy content more than raw speed. Describing only “it calls GPT to write a course”. Purpose.
