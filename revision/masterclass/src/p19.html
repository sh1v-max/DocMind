Critical minimums

Even with a good overall score, the interviewer looks for:

  • Backend development: at least 6/10.
  • SQL and database fundamentals: at least 5/10.
  • RAG: at least 10/15.
  • Practical coding: at least 6/10.
  • Genuine ownership of at least one substantial project.

19.4What a fresher can realistically aim for

A strong fresher target with this course: 80+, with RAG ≥ 12/15 and backend ≥ 8/10, because those are where DocMind gives you real depth. Weakest areas for you right now are likely “practical coding” under time pressure (practise Phase 11 with a timer) and “project ownership” questions about tests and production readiness (have the technical-debt answer ready: Phase 13.48).
