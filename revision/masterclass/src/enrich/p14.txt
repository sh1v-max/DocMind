@Q 14.10

@Q 14.11

@Q 14.15
S: The failing step retries according to its policy; transient errors usually pass. If it still fails, the run fails with completed steps’ results and drafts preserved; the course is marked failed with a reason; a retry re-runs the workflow with the same course key, and idempotent steps skip finished work so only the failed part is redone.
Q: Should one bad lesson fail the whole course?
A: By default yes (publish requires completeness), but you could mark it and let the user regenerate just that lesson.
N: Restarting from scratch.
@Q 14.16
S: Two layers, chosen deliberately: transport retries in the LLM client (429/5xx/timeouts with backoff and jitter, honouring Retry-After), and step retries for invalid outputs or remaining failures (step retries: 2). Validation errors on input are never retried. Total attempts per item are bounded, logged, and visible in cost dashboards.
Q: How do you avoid retry multiplication?
A: Count the layers (client 2 × step 2 = 4 calls max per lesson) and keep each small.
N: Infinite retries.
@Q 14.17
S: Duplicates come from repeated submissions, double approvals, retries and recovery re-runs.
